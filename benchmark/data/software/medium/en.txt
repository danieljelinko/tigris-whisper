At two fourteen in the morning on Tuesday, the on-call engineer received a page indicating that the checkout service was returning errors for about five percent of requests. The dashboard showed that the database connection pool had reached its maximum of one hundred connections, and latency on the payments endpoint had increased from two hundred milliseconds to over four seconds. The engineer restarted three pods in the checkout namespace, which temporarily reduced the error rate, but the connection pool filled up again within eight minutes, so the team escalated to the database administrators on call.
